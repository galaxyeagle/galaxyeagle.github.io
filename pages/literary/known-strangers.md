Life is unfair. 
Only God knows why we briefly met in this life. 
And then the other moment everything's gone. 
Like a passing glimpse in a moving crowd. 
What was his intention? 
To start a stream 
but dry it out ere reaching the far-away sea? 
I sometimes wake up in the morning 
thinking of texting you 
about my projects, 
my workplace tidbits, 
my plans for our future. 
And suddenly, 
I grasp the harsh reality,
blinded by the ruthless Sun. 
It's over. 
Scarcely can I can grieve, 
when the busy day takes over. 
You start calling me a known stranger 
e'en prior to parting ways,
like burying a man while still alive.
And before I realise, 
you are halfway someone else's. 
But was it really a right decision? 
Were we really not meant for each other 
even though our vibes and thoughts matched so well? 
Or is it just easy for you to make vibes match 
with anyone who crosses your threshold? 
I don't know. 
But sometimes those flashbacks touch the nerve 
of sweet convos, 
now lost in the ruins of past.
Now that we've both moved on, 
can you promise you won't leave me 
in another life?
