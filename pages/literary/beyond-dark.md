title:  Beyond the dark
date:   2026-10-29
---

I've seen the night descend upon the earth,
as though the sky had judged us and found us wanting.
I've heard the wind move through the empty streets,
carrying the names of things
no living mouth would dare pronounce.

And I've seen men grow old
before their time;
not from the passing of the years,
but from slow surrender of wonder.

What strange machinery is this,
that teaches hearts to beat
yet gives them strings to pull it dead ?

We enter life with nothing in our hands,
and spend our years defending what we gather.
Then death arrives, as quiet as a thought,
And sweeps the merriment away.

What were the fights and counter-arguments for ?
What were all those sleepless nights
in which the mind became its own executioner?

The world keeps turning.

It turns above the ruins of our certainties,
Above the graves of men who thought themselves eternal,
Above the fields where nameless bones
have fed the grass that feeds the next generation.

Pain has taught the world enough.

It has taught the strong to fear their weakness,
The weak to hide their strength,
And generations to mistake the things they suffered
For the things they must pass on.

But suffering is not sacred simply because it lasts.
A chain is still a chain when worn for centuries.
A lie does not become the truth
Because a thousand fathers taught it to their sons.

Let the old ghosts speak.

Let them fill the corridors with all the voices
That once commanded kingdoms,
Let them summon every failure from its grave
And lay the evidence before the living.

I have no need to silence history.

I need only deny it the right
To call itself the future.

For there is a difference
Between remembering the fire
And choosing to burn forever.

And somewhere, beyond the reach of all our grief,
The world is opening into morning.

Not a perfect world.
Not one cleansed of greed, betrayal, or human madness.
The same old earth, with all its scars,
Still turning through the cold, indifferent heavens.

Life has simply refused
To make its suffering the whole of its story.

I have no bargain with the heavens.
No promise that tomorrow will be kind.

Tomorrow may arrive with fire in its hands.
The road may vanish underneath my feet.
The world may ask for more than I can give.

Let those who worship darkness call it wisdom.
Let those who fear the end spend all their lives preparing for it.

I have seen enough of death
To know that life deserves more than fear.

There is a world beyond the wound,
A sky beyond the smoke,
A thousand mornings waiting in the dark.
The steed of hope is broke and unbridled.

And so I mock the deep darkness
that threatens to swallow me.
It will fall off.
For I know my heart,
which braves but never snaps.
